How far in advance should I book?

Call us whatever your dates are — we handle last-minute interstate moves regularly. If you're planning ahead, 4–6 weeks is comfortable, and 6–8 weeks in peak season (May through August).

How is the quote calculated?

It's a fixed price, based on the weight of your shipment, the miles between origin and destination, and the services you choose. We survey by video or in person first, then put the number in writing.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Fully licensed, bonded, and insured with $1,000,000 in general liability coverage, and FMCSA-registered for interstate moves across all continental states. USDOT #4575745 · MC-1820728.

What happens if something breaks?

Damage happens in less than 0.3% of our moves — and when it does, we take full responsibility, file promptly, and follow through until it's resolved.

Do you move pianos, safes, and unusual items?

Yes — pianos, gun safes, hot tubs, pool tables, art and antiques. Mention it when you book so we can plan the crew and gear.
