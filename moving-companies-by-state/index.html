Most-requested destinations.

Where the country is moving right now. Each guide covers arrival logistics, building and HOA rules, and how long the lane takes.

01

Moving to Texas

Houston, Dallas, San Antonio, Austin and beyond. Texas leads the country in net inbound migration, and the largest single source is California, followed by Florida, Illinois, New York, and Louisiana.

02

Moving to Florida

Miami, Orlando, Tampa, Jacksonville and beyond. Florida's inbound flow is enormous and comes primarily from New York, New Jersey, Illinois, Pennsylvania, and Ohio, concentrated in Tampa Bay, Southwest Florida, and Central Florida.

03

Moving to California

Los Angeles, San Diego, San Jose, San Francisco and beyond. The dominant outbound lanes from California are Texas, Arizona, Nevada, Washington, Idaho, and Florida, and they are driven overwhelmingly by housing cost and remote-work flexibility.

04

Moving to North Carolina

Charlotte, Raleigh, Greensboro, Durham and beyond. North Carolina gains population from almost every direction.

05

Moving to Arizona

Phoenix, Tucson, Mesa, Chandler and beyond. Maricopa County has been adding population faster than almost any other county in the US, and the largest single inbound stream is still Southern California — households trading coastal housing costs for Chandler, Gilbert, and Surprise.

06

Moving to Georgia

Atlanta, Savannah, Augusta, Columbus and beyond. Metro Atlanta has been a top-five inbound metro nationally for years, drawing households from New York, Illinois, California, and the surrounding Southeast for jobs in film, logistics, and corporate headquarters.
